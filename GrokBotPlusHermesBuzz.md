Grok Bot + Hermes is the most powerful agentic setup that exists right now.

Most people use one or the other, but the real power comes from stacking the tools together.

Here's how to combine these two AI tools for maximum productivity:

Why they're actually complementary, not competitors (my experience)

Grok Bot gives you a cloud computer per agent, sign-ins into any app, model flexibility (Opus, Claude, GPT-5.6), and a slick plug-and-play setup.

Hermes gives you full data sovereignty, self-hosted memory you own outright, and a $5-10/month cost floor instead of a $120-300/month one.

The split I'm currently running:

→ Use Grok Bot for anything that needs deep app integration: Salesforce, Slack, Gmail, Figma, my CRMs. It's built for signing into tools and working across your entire stack.

→ Use Hermes for anything sensitive, cost-heavy, or where you want full control of the memory. Financial data, personal admin, anything running 24/7 where you don't want to pay premium pricing for volume.

How to actually connect them

I'm connecting both AI agents through Buzz, Jack Dorsey's open-source team chat.

Add your Grok Bot agents and your Hermes agents to the same workspace, and they can coordinate directly in shared channels instead of operating in silos.

Alternatively, have your Grok Bot Chief of Staff delegate specific tasks to Hermes via a webhook or shared task file, using Grok for the app-heavy orchestration layer and Hermes as a cheap, always-on execution layer underneath it.

A real setup example

→ Grok Bot handles your GTM stack: meeting prep, inbox triage, slide generation, CRM updates
→ Hermes runs your personal financial research, subscription audits, and content research in the background, dramatically cheaper per task
→ Both report into the same Buzz workspace, so you check one place instead of switching between two apps
